    LET YOUR VOCABULARY GROW

    How to play

    Find 12 different words using only the seven letters in your flower. There's no time limit.

    1. Use at least four letters and include the purple center letter in every word.
    2. Letters can be used more than once. Shuffle only rearranges the six outer petals.
    3. The yellow-outlined letter earns 5 extra points every time it appears in an accepted word.
    4. Use all seven letters at least once for a pangram: 7 extra points.
    5. Only words in this game's approved English list count. No proper nouns, hyphens, foul language, or repeated submissions. Invalid words don't cost a slot.
